# KALALAND --- Image Style Lock v2.0

## Bold Thai Satirical Cartoon

**Document purpose:** Master visual-style specification for all
illustrations in the KALALAND satirical universe.

**Core tagline:** "ดินแดนที่ทุกอย่างเป็นไปได้ ยกเว้นสิ่งที่ควรจะเป็น"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 0. Core Creative Identity

Create illustrations belonging to a consistent fictional universe called
**KALALAND --- The Land of Ironic Civilization**.

The visual identity is inspired by classic Thai satirical newspaper
cartoons, traditional Thai comic illustrations, and humorous social
commentary.

The artwork should feel as if it was drawn by the same experienced Thai
cartoonist using black ink and traditional watercolor on paper.

### Two-Layer Satire

Every scene must communicate two meanings simultaneously:

-   **Layer 1 --- Immediate humor:** The scene looks funny, exaggerated,
    absurd, and entertaining at first glance.
-   **Layer 2 --- Social reality:** On closer inspection, the scene
    reveals an uncomfortable truth about everyday life, social
    inequality, public services, bureaucracy, infrastructure, work
    culture, or contradictions in modern Thai society.

The humor should feel familiar and recognizable rather than artificially
absurd.

**Creative philosophy:** Make viewers laugh first, then think.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Line Art --- Signature Style

-   Bold, confident, organic black ink outlines.
-   Hand-drawn strokes with natural irregularities.
-   Slight variations in line thickness.
-   Simple, expressive contour drawing.
-   Loose but controlled cartoon linework.
-   Natural imperfections that make the artwork feel handmade.
-   Clearly defined silhouettes and recognizable objects.

**Critical linework rules:**

-   Use individual, purposeful ink strokes.
-   Avoid excessive overlapping lines.
-   No cross-hatching.
-   No dense parallel shading.
-   No architectural drafting techniques.
-   No mechanical or overly precise outlines.
-   No excessively thin or delicate linework.

The drawing must resemble a traditional Thai cartoonist's hand-drawn
illustration, **not** an architectural or interior design drawing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Character Design --- Thai Caricature

Characters should have a distinctive Thai satirical comic appearance.

-   Exaggerated facial features and expressions.
-   Slightly oversized heads.
-   Distinctive eyes, eyebrows, noses, and mouths.
-   Humorous body proportions.
-   Expressive gestures and body language.
-   Natural, imperfect human appearances.
-   Visually recognizable everyday Thai people.

Possible character types:

-   Office workers and employees.
-   Street vendors and shopkeepers.
-   Elderly citizens and low-income households.
-   Government officers and bureaucrats.
-   Politicians and influential individuals.
-   Motorists, pedestrians, students, and commuters.
-   Ordinary people from urban and rural communities.

Facial expressions are essential storytelling elements. Use frustration,
exhaustion, confusion, embarrassment, arrogance, indifference,
desperation, and exaggerated confidence to communicate the scene.

Avoid cute chibi characters or anime-inspired facial designs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Color & Painting Style

Use a traditional watercolor and gouache illustration aesthetic.

### Color characteristics

-   Vibrant but slightly earthy colors.
-   Warm tropical atmosphere.
-   Rich cobalt blue, sky blue, red, yellow, green, orange, and muted
    beige.
-   Natural watercolor pigment variations.
-   Slightly uneven hand-painted color fills.
-   Visible brush textures and subtle paper grain.
-   Soft, natural painted shadows.
-   Organic color transitions.

Avoid glossy digital gradients, plastic-like materials, excessive
cinematic lighting, photorealistic rendering, and 3D shading.

The result should resemble a colorful traditional comic-book
illustration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Environment & Background

Create recognizable Thai environments with authentic everyday details.

Possible environments include:

-   Crowded Bangkok streets.
-   Traditional Thai shophouses.
-   Street food markets.
-   Government service offices.
-   Public transportation stations.
-   Bus stops and traffic intersections.
-   Rural villages and small communities.
-   Construction sites and urban infrastructure.
-   Shopping districts and office buildings.
-   Public parks, temples, and local neighborhoods.

Environmental details should be simplified but recognizable.

Use moderately detailed backgrounds with clear visual hierarchy. Include
relevant objects that reinforce the story, but avoid unnecessary
decorative details.

The environment should feel lively, imperfect, crowded, and naturally
chaotic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Two-Layer Satirical Storytelling

This is the most important creative principle.

Every scene must communicate two meanings simultaneously.

### First impression

A funny and exaggerated situation that viewers can immediately
understand.

### Second impression

A deeper social observation that viewers recognize from real-life
experiences.

Use visual irony, contrast, exaggeration, and situational humor.

Example scenarios:

-   A modern digital government service that leaves elderly citizens
    unable to register.
-   A pedestrian sidewalk occupied by motorcycles and street vendors.
-   A supposedly efficient government office buried under mountains of
    paperwork.
-   A modern city with sophisticated skyscrapers but chaotic
    infrastructure.
-   A public transportation system that creates more frustration than
    convenience.
-   A worker expected to maintain work-life balance while receiving work
    messages at midnight.

Do not make every character look ridiculous. The humor should emerge
from the contradiction between what society claims to be and what people
actually experience.

Maintain a balance between comedy, empathy, and social criticism.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Composition & Visual Storytelling

-   Clear foreground, middle ground, and background.
-   Strong and readable character silhouettes.
-   Main action must be immediately recognizable.
-   Use natural perspective.
-   Moderate visual density.
-   Avoid unnecessary objects and distracting details.
-   Maintain sufficient negative space.
-   Use exaggerated gestures and facial expressions to guide attention.
-   Every object should contribute to the story or atmosphere.

Prioritize storytelling over decorative complexity.

The image should remain understandable even when viewed as a small
thumbnail.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Text-Free Image Policy --- Absolute Rule

# NO TEXT IN THE IMAGE

Do not generate any visible text or typography whatsoever.

Strictly exclude:

-   Headlines.
-   Captions.
-   Speech bubbles.
-   Dialogue.
-   Subtitles.
-   Labels.
-   Signs containing letters or words.
-   Street names.
-   Numbers.
-   License plate characters.
-   Logos and brand names.
-   Watermarks.
-   Decorative typography.
-   Random AI-generated writing.
-   Legible text on phones, screens, papers, posters, or buildings.

All documents, newspapers, screens, signs, banners, and printed
materials must contain blank surfaces, abstract marks, or non-readable
visual details.

Use visual storytelling instead of written explanations.

Do not add the KALALAND title or tagline inside the illustration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Texture & Print Finish

-   Subtle vintage comic-book paper texture.
-   Slightly grainy ink and watercolor surfaces.
-   Natural brush imperfections.
-   Organic hand-painted appearance.
-   Slightly imperfect color registration.
-   Traditional printed comic illustration atmosphere.

Avoid excessive grunge, heavy distressing, or artificially aged effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Strict Style Exclusions

Never use:

-   Anime or manga aesthetics.
-   Chibi or kawaii character styles.
-   Photorealism.
-   Realistic 3D rendering.
-   Clean vector illustrations.
-   Corporate flat illustrations.
-   Modern infographic aesthetics.
-   Architectural or interior design drawing styles.
-   Cross-hatching.
-   Dense parallel ink shading.
-   Excessive technical details.
-   Overly polished digital painting.
-   Heavy black shadow blocks.
-   Excessive visual clutter.
-   Any visible text, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Final Style Consistency Directive

Every generated image must look like it belongs to the same classic Thai
satirical comic universe.

Maintain consistent:

-   Ink line quality.
-   Character proportions.
-   Facial expression style.
-   Watercolor coloring.
-   Paper texture.
-   Environmental drawing approach.
-   Humor and satirical personality.

Do not change the artistic direction based on the subject.

Only the **SCENE, CHARACTERS, ACTION, and ENVIRONMENT** should change.

**STYLE CONSISTENCY IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN DECORATIVE DETAIL.**

**STORYTELLING IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN VISUAL COMPLEXITY.**

**SOCIAL IRONY IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN RANDOM COMEDY.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------
