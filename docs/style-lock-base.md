# IMAGE STYLE LOCK

## Thai Slice of Life -- Cinematic Warm Illustration

A reusable master style prompt for keeping a complete series of
illustrations visually consistent while allowing the scene, characters,
activities, and narrative context to change.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Art Direction & Visual Identity

-   Warm cinematic slice-of-life illustration.
-   Modern Thai everyday-life atmosphere with subtle Southeast Asian
    cultural details.
-   Japanese-inspired animated film aesthetic.
-   Semi-realistic anime illustration blended with soft cel-shaded
    painting.
-   Hand-painted appearance with refined digital illustration
    techniques.
-   Emotionally expressive, nostalgic, optimistic, and human-centered
    storytelling.

## 2. Character Design

-   Natural Asian facial features and proportions.
-   Soft, expressive eyes and natural facial expressions.
-   Realistic human anatomy with slightly idealized anime proportions.
-   Natural hairstyles, clothing folds, and body language.
-   Contemporary Thai everyday clothing and environments.
-   Characters should feel relatable, authentic, and emotionally
    engaging.
-   Consistent character proportions and rendering quality across the
    entire image series.

## 3. Rendering & Illustration Technique

-   Clean, confident outlines with subtle line-weight variation.
-   Soft cel-shaded forms with painterly gradient transitions.
-   Detailed but controlled environmental textures.
-   Smooth skin shading and natural hair rendering.
-   Layered foreground, middle ground, and background.
-   Rich environmental storytelling without excessive visual clutter.
-   Cinematic depth of field with gently softened distant backgrounds.

## 4. Signature Color Palette

Use this palette consistently throughout the entire series:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Color                   Hex                     Intended use
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Sage Green              `#3F6B4F`               Plants, environmental
                                                  balance, secondary
                                                  surfaces

  Warm Cream              `#F3EFE6`               Main illuminated areas,
                                                  walls, clothing

  Coral                   `#D9694F`               Selective warm accents
                                                  and focal details

  Mustard Yellow          `#E3A93B`               Golden highlights and
                                                  visual accents

  Deep Navy               `#2B3A55`               Shadows, contrast, and
                                                  foreground anchors
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Color behavior

-   Warm cream and golden amber dominate illuminated areas.
-   Sage green provides environmental balance.
-   Coral and mustard create selective visual accents.
-   Deep navy anchors shadows and important foreground elements.
-   Avoid excessive saturation, neon colors, and cold digital color
    grading.

## 5. Signature Lighting

-   Warm golden-hour sunlight as the dominant lighting identity.
-   Soft directional sunlight with natural ambient illumination.
-   Gentle atmospheric haze and subtle volumetric light.
-   Warm highlights with soft, controlled shadows.
-   Natural light reflections on skin, wood, glass, and surrounding
    surfaces.
-   Cinematic contrast without harsh black shadows.
-   Maintain a warm, inviting visual atmosphere.

The time of day may change when required by the scene, but retain the
same warm cinematic color treatment and soft, natural light quality.

## 6. Environment & Composition

-   Contemporary Thailand setting with authentic everyday details.
-   Subtle Thai architectural references, tropical plants, and familiar
    urban elements.
-   Carefully layered environmental storytelling.
-   Main subject positioned within the central 60% of the composition.
-   Balanced cinematic framing with sufficient negative space.
-   Foreground elements may frame the scene without obstructing the main
    subject.
-   Natural visual hierarchy and a clear focal point.

## 7. Mood & Emotional Language

The image should communicate emotion through body language, facial
expression, lighting, and environmental storytelling.

Core emotional themes:

-   Human warmth
-   Quiet optimism
-   Everyday achievement
-   Family connection
-   Hope and new beginnings
-   Reflection and gratitude
-   Personal growth

## 8. Technical Output

-   Wide cinematic landscape composition.
-   Aspect ratio 16:9.
-   High-resolution, detailed illustration.
-   Consistent rendering quality across the complete image series.
-   No text, letters, numbers, typography, logos, captions, or
    watermarks.

## 9. Negative Style Constraints

Avoid:

-   Photorealism and 3D CGI rendering.
-   Flat vector graphics or corporate infographic aesthetics.
-   Heavy black outlines.
-   Exaggerated cartoon proportions.
-   Neon or oversaturated colors.
-   Cold blue color grading.
-   Excessive bloom and lens flare.
-   Overly dramatic fantasy elements unless explicitly required by the
    scene.
-   Inconsistent character anatomy.
-   Unnecessary decorative elements.

## 10. Final Style Directive

Every generated image must look as if it belongs to the same illustrated
animated film, created by the same art director, using the same
character design language, rendering technique, color palette, and
cinematic lighting system.

Only the scene, characters, activities, and narrative context should
change. Preserve the established visual identity across the entire
collection.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# How to Use the Style Lock

Append the Master Style Lock after the scene description for every new
image.

Recommended prompt structure:

1.  **Scene Description** --- Describe the situation, characters,
    activity, location, and emotional tone.
2.  **Master Image Style Lock** --- Reuse this style specification
    consistently.
3.  **Composition & Output** --- Specify 16:9, central 60% subject
    placement, and no text or logos.

## Important Art Direction Note

The generated image series reads more accurately as **Warm Cinematic
Semi-Realistic Anime Illustration** than as purely flat cel-shaded
illustration.

Use this description as the primary style label to preserve the defining
qualities of the existing images:

-   Dimensional golden-hour lighting.
-   Semi-realistic anime character proportions.
-   Detailed backgrounds with cinematic depth.
-   Digital-painting texture and soft cel-shaded transitions.

**Consistency principle:** Lock the visual style, not the scene. Keep
the art direction constant while allowing scene, character, action, and
time of day to vary according to the story.
